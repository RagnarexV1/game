Yes, it was a bug, and I've fixed it. Typecheck passes, but I haven't checked it in the game.

Cause: whenever Sas stepped through a door, the code swapped the door tile to door_open. The drawing code shows door_open as the grass sprite, and only door_closed gets the door sprite on a floor or white-tile background. That swap wasn't needed anyway: the player and monsters already walk through door_closed. It happened both on the walk to the horse and, since my last change, whenever Sas walks through a door to catch up with you. That's why the hall doors turned to grass.

Fix in GameScene.ts:

Both places where Sas swapped the door are removed, so doors stay door_closed.
Existing saves get repaired: when a map is restored from a save, any door_open tile is turned back into door_closed. Your hall maps should show their doors again the next time you enter them.
